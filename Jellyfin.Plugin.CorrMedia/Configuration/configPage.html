Which sidecar treatments to apply

Default. Run every sidecar edit as written (mute, beep, crop, skip, and so on). Turn this off to choose categories. These controls only turn treatments on or off; they do not change how a treatment is done. Restart playback after saving.

The chevron opens a category. The camera or speaker turns that class of sidecar treatment on or off. The sidecar still decides mute vs beep vs crop vs skip. Drill into Language to target specific words. Unknown sidecar tags count as Other.
